How to interpret

Price above EMA can indicate upward momentum; below EMA can indicate downward momentum. RSI above 70 or below 30 can indicate stretched momentum, not an automatic reversal.

Use enough closing prices for the selected periods. These calculations do not detect BOS, CHOCH, FVG or order blocks automatically.
