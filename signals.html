Rules used

BUY: EMA 20 above EMA 50, with RSI above 50 and below 70.

SELL: EMA 20 below EMA 50, with RSI below 50 and above 30.

If neither rule is met, the planner reports NO TRADE. These are basic educational rules, not a proven profitable strategy.

Before trading

The entry is the price you enter. The stop-loss is placed 1.5 ATR away by default, and the target is calculated from your selected reward-to-risk ratio. This page does not execute trades, fetch live prices, check spread, or account for slippage. Confirm all levels on your broker's chart before use.

A displayed setup is not a recommendation to risk real money. Test on a demo account first.
